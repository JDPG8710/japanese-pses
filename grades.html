Choose a starting point for school-year practice

Piko Game groups supplementary activities by familiar school-year labels. Select your country or region, a school system and a year, then choose an available subject. Start at the first open gate; later stages unlock as you complete the earlier ones.

What the labels mean

Year names help you navigate; they do not establish equivalent attainment between countries. A Reception year, Kindergarten and a numbered primary year can cover different skills. Choose the label used by your school, then start with a topic the learner already recognises. The paths cover selected practice skills, not every part of a national curriculum.

Choose practice that fits

If a task feels too difficult, return to free play and try the first difficulty level. Explain one step before repeating a round. Local completion records describe game attempts; they are not school grades. Only subjects with available activities appear in a selected year's map.

The interactive selector loads here. You can also start from the country selector, open free-choice logic games, or read a guide below.
