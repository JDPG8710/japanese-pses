# Content policy (engineering constraints)

## China primary materials

- Do **not** merge unauthorized Chinese textbook body text, passages, or illustrations (e.g. 人教版课文原文) into this repository or production content.
- `docs/curriculum/CHINA_PRIMARY_OUTLINE.md` is research/design only until licensed materials and required domestic qualifications exist.
- Marketing must not claim “official textbook / 提分培训 / 教育部认定” for China paths.
- Prefer original drills, public-domain references, and clearly labeled original summaries.

## Japan curriculum data

- Kanji / prefecture / curriculum JSON titles must state that content is **compiled with reference to** public MEXT materials, and is **not** official, supervised, or a full database republication.
- Forbidden marketing phrases in product copy: `官方认证`, `教育部认定`, and endorsement-style `监修` / full-archive marketing claims (as endorsement). Allowed: `参照`, `対応学年`.

## Ads and child direction

- Optional ads load only after privacy consent **and** parental gate, and never under CN safe mode or EEA/UK/CH without a certified CMP.
