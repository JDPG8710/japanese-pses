PRIVACY NOTICE

How Piko Play handles information

Piko Play is an educational game for primary-school learners. Playing does not require an account. This notice describes the service as of 5 September 2026.

Information kept on the device

The selected country or region, interface language, learner nickname, game progress, settings and practice results may be stored in the browser. Clearing site data removes this local information. The connection country is used only to suggest a starting choice and is not added to a server profile by that suggestion alone.

Optional sign-in and cloud saving

If a parent, guardian or other eligible user chooses Google sign-in, Piko Play receives the Google account identifier, display name and email address needed to create and secure the account. Cloud saving can also include a learner nickname, age range, selected gender value, progress, scores, attempts, rewards and certificates. A secure session cookie lasts up to seven days. Public rankings use only a system-generated explorer name and score; they do not display a real name or email address.

Payments, advertising and security providers

If paid membership is activated, Stripe processes payment details. Piko Play stores payment event and checkout identifiers and the membership result, but not full card numbers. Google AdSense may serve ads. The site marks ad requests for child-directed and under-age treatment so personalized advertising and remarketing are not used. Cloudflare provides hosting, D1 storage, security and Turnstile bot protection and may process IP address, device and request information for those purposes.

Why information is used

  • to provide games, save progress and show rankings;
  • to authenticate accounts and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • to provide membership and payment support where enabled;
  • to operate, troubleshoot and secure the service.

Piko Play does not sell personal information.

Children and family choices

Children can play without signing in. A parent or guardian should manage any sign-in, cloud profile or purchase for a child and should avoid entering a child’s full legal name. Requests to access, correct or delete cloud account information can be sent through the developer support contact shown on the Google sign-in consent screen. Local information can be removed by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Service providers and international processing

Google, Cloudflare and Stripe process information under their own privacy terms and may process it in countries outside the user’s country. Only information needed for the functions described above is shared with them.
