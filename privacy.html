PRIVACY NOTICE

How Piko Game handles information

Piko Game is an educational game for primary-school learners. Playing does not require an account. This notice describes the service as of 6 September 2026.

Information kept on the device

The selected country or region, interface language, learner nickname, game progress, settings and practice results may be stored in the browser. Clearing site data removes this local information. The connection country is used only to suggest a starting choice and is not added to a server profile by that suggestion alone.

Cookies, local storage and your choice

Necessary browser storage supports security, a sign-in you request, country and language choices, sound settings and learning progress. The OAuth security cookie is short-lived. A signed-in session cookie lasts up to seven days. The privacy choice itself is remembered on this device for up to 180 days so the site does not ask on every visit.

Google advertising code is optional and is not loaded unless a parent or guardian selects “allow optional ads”. If allowed, Google may use cookies or similar storage to provide and measure child-directed, non-personalised advertising. You can withdraw or change that choice at any time. Advertising remains disabled for visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until a Google-certified consent platform is configured.

Optional sign-in and cloud saving

If a parent, guardian or other eligible user chooses Google sign-in, Piko Game receives the Google account identifier, display name and email address needed to create and secure the account. Cloud saving can also include a learner nickname, age range, selected gender value, progress, scores, attempts, rewards and certificates. A secure session cookie lasts up to seven days. Public rankings use only a system-generated explorer name and score; they do not display a real name or email address.

Payments, advertising and security providers

If paid membership is activated, Stripe processes payment details. Piko Game stores payment event and checkout identifiers and the membership result, but not full card numbers. With the optional advertising choice, Google AdSense may serve non-personalised ads. The site marks ad requests for child-directed and under-age treatment, and does not enable personalised advertising or remarketing. Cloudflare provides hosting, D1 storage, security and Turnstile bot protection and may process IP address, device and request information for those purposes. Turnstile is loaded only when a user opens the sign-in flow.

Why information is used

  • to provide games, save progress and show rankings;
  • to authenticate accounts and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • to provide membership and payment support where enabled;
  • to operate, troubleshoot and secure the service.

Piko Game does not sell personal information.

Children and family choices

Children can play without signing in. A parent or guardian should manage any privacy choice, sign-in, cloud profile or purchase for a child and should avoid entering a child’s full legal name. Requests to access, correct or delete cloud account information can be sent to the contact address below. Local information can be removed by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Service providers and international processing

Google, Cloudflare and Stripe process information under their own privacy terms and may process it in countries outside the user’s country. Only information needed for the functions described above is shared with them.

Online Go rooms / 在线围棋 / オンライン囲碁

The server stores moves, clocks, results and participant identifiers to run and restore games; completed games are archived. Signed-in games are linked to your account for history. Opponents see a generated alias, not your account name or email. Anyone with an invitation link can request the open seat. Short-lived request limits use a hash of the connection IP address.
