A future-facing learning vision

Inspired by the direction of the OECD Learning Compass 2030

Piko Game’s product philosophy echoes the Learning Compass emphasis on student agency and the ability to navigate unfamiliar situations. Games invite children to anticipate, act, observe feedback and try a better route.

Reconciling tensionsCompare constraints, clues and competing possibilities.

Piko Game is an independent educational product. It is not affiliated with, certified by or endorsed by the OECD. Read the official OECD Learning Compass overview.

Education equity

A smaller barrier can become a bigger beginning

Educational equity is a direction, not a slogan. Piko Game reduces common entry barriers through free-to-start browser access, multilingual navigation and optional accounts.

  • Access before registration. Core play starts without asking a child to create an account.
  • Local progress. Practice can be saved on the device; optional sign-in supports cloud features.
  • More than one cultural starting point. Country, language and school-year choices acknowledge that learners arrive with different contexts.

Questions from families

Good to know before you play

Is Piko Game free?

Children can start core browser games for free. An account is optional. Additional paid features may be offered only where enabled and will be shown before purchase.

Which ages is it designed for?

Piko Game is designed as supplementary practice for primary-school learners. Adults should choose a suitable school-year path and supervise younger children where appropriate.

Does Piko Game use an official OECD curriculum?

No. The product philosophy is informed by ideas such as learner agency and future-oriented competencies, but Piko Game is independent and is not an OECD curriculum, partner, certification or endorsement.

What languages and education systems are available?

The interface supports English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. School-year practice includes paths labelled for China, the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand and international practice, plus a dedicated Japanese primary course.
